# CONCEPT — Ze Simulator

**Version:** 1.0

## What it is
Rust simulator of Ze Theory — a quantum-information model of wave function collapse and verification of Tsirelson bound (bound CHSH ≤ 2√2). Numerical experiment for validation of Ze axioms.

## Why
- Confirm predictions of Ze Theory through numerical simulation
- Verify Tsirelson bound within the Z₂-gauge model
- Reproduce Born rule from Ze axioms

## How it works
- **ze-core** — library with physical model
- **ze-runner** — binary that runs the simulation
- **bootstrap_vstar_results.json** — reference results for comparison

## Status
Initial stage. Basic CHSH simulation works, expansion to Ze_D and Ze-Hierarchy required.
