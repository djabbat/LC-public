I am putting together an EIC Pathfinder consortium (deadline 28 October 2026) and would like to ask whether the Epigenetic Counter role fits your group.

**The project — MCARA:** Five independent counters of replicative aging, measured in parallel on the same cells. Human fibroblasts (BJ-hTERT, 2% O₂). Active telomerase, low oxygen — telomeres and mitochondria are protected, yet the cells eventually arrest. We think the centriole is the limiting factor, but all five counters must be measured to prove it.

**C2 — Epigenetic Counter:** DNA methylation clock (Illumina EPIC) on the same cells at three passage points (P25, P35, P45) and at arrest. Your clock calibrates the time axis. Without it, we cannot say which counter runs out first.
