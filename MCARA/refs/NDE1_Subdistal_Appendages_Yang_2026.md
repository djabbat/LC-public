## MCARA Relevance: Subdistal Appendage Layering + Centriole→Autophagy Link

### Why This Matters

NDE1 — a dynein/ciliogenesis regulator — is a **ring-like component of the subdistal appendages**, sitting between CEP128 and ninein. Depleting it:
- breaks centrosome integrity (centriole pair separation, ectopic foci)
- disrupts PCM and MTOC organization
- 🔴 **raises LC3B/p62 and lowers autophagic flux** — a direct centriole→autophagy bridge

### SDA Molecular Layering (3 quantitative markers)

| Layer | Protein | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Proximal | **CEP128** | ODF2/CEP128-dependent branch |
| Middle | **NDE1** | ring, depends on ODF2+CEP128 |
| Peripheral | **ninein** | outer layer |

NDE1 is in the ODF2/CEP128 branch — **NOT** the CEP170 branch (CEP170 branch → DSB repair, Rodríguez-Real 2023, PMID 37664992).

### Supporting the "Two Functions" Hypothesis (Tqemaladze 2025)

- Distal appendages → cilia signaling
- **Subdistal appendages → MTOC organization** ← NDE1 depletion phenocopies MTOC-branch failure
