## MCARA Relevance: Clathrin as Model Multi-Function Counter

### Why This Matters

Clathrin is the best-characterized **moonlighting protein** — a single protein with two independent, mechanistically distinct functions:

| Function | Cell Cycle Phase | Mechanism |
|----------|:---------------:|-----------|
| Endocytic coat | Interphase | Vesicle formation at plasma membrane |
| Spindle stabilizer | Mitosis | 4+ mechanisms (below) |

### Four Independent Mitotic Mechanisms (one protein → multiple counters)

1. **Inter-microtubule bridging** — TACC3/chTOG/clathrin complex crosslinks kinetochore fibers (Booth 2011, Nixon 2015)
2. **GTSE1 recruitment** — CHC adaptor sites bind GTSE1 → MCAK inhibition on astral MTs (Rondelet 2020)
3. **Centrosome integrity** — Clathrin stabilizes centrosomal ch-TOG (Foraker 2012) 🔴
4. **CHC-pT606 signaling** — GAK→CHC→PLK1→Kiz axis at centrosome (Yabuno 2019) 🔴

**This is a paradigm for MCARA:** one protein providing multiple counters through different molecular mechanisms.
