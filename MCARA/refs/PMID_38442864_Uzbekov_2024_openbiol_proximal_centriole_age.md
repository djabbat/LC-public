# Uzbekov R, Avidor-Reiss T (2024)
## The proximal centriole age in spermatozoa is a potential reason for its different fate in the zygote after fertilization

**Journal:** Open Biology 14:230458
**PMID:** 38442864

**Key finding:** The age of the proximal centriole in spermatozoa determines whether it is eliminated or retained in the zygote. Older centrioles are preferentially eliminated — providing evidence for age-dependent centriole selection during the germline reset.

**MCARA relevance:** Directly supports the centriole age hypothesis. The elimination machinery discriminates centrioles by age — older = targeted for removal. This is consistent with the morphogenetic lock model: the cell can detect centriole age and eliminate aged organelles while retaining young ones.
