## CEDAR-Relevant Findings

### 🔴 Direct Connection: Clathrin Stabilizes Centrosomes
**Foraker et al., 2012, J Cell Biol** (PMID 22891263, 591 цит.):
- Clathrin depletion → centrosome amplification + multipolar spindles
- Clathrin stabilizes **centrosomal ch-TOG** during centrosome maturation
- Acute clathrin inactivation in S phase → reduced ch-TOG at metaphase centrosomes → centrosome fragmentation
- Phenocopied by Aurora A kinase inhibitor

**Implication for CEDAR:**
- Centrosome = centriole-containing organelle. Clathrin maintains its integrity.
- Age-dependent loss of clathrin function → centrosome instability → multipolar spindles → aneuploidy → aging
- This is a **testable prediction**: CHC-pT606 levels at centrosomes in young vs aged cells

### CHC Phosphorylation at Centrosome
**Yabuno et al., 2019, Cell Cycle** (PMID 31272276):
- CHC phosphorylated at T606 by GAK kinase
- CHC-pT606 localizes to nucleus and **centrosome** in interphase
- Forms complex: GAK → CHC-pT606 → PLK1 → Kiz-pT379
- GAK depletion → metaphase arrest

### Centrosome Connection in Cancer
**Godinho & Pellman, 2014, Phil Trans R Soc B** (365 цит.):
- Centrosome abnormalities → chromosomal instability → cancer

**Saatci et al., 2022** — TACC3 represents vulnerability in breast cancers with centrosome amplification
