## Key content
- **TRIM37** loss/deficiency creates a **cancer-specific vulnerability to PLK4 inhibition** — a synthetic-lethality paradigm.
- PLK4 is the master kinase of centriole duplication; its inhibition kills cells with centrosome defects while sparing normal cells.
