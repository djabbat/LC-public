## Key content
- **Direct evidence** that centrosome aberrations are hallmarks of cancer AND contribute to the **senescence process**.
- Structural/numerical centrosome abnormalities → mitotic errors, cellular senescence, cell death, genomic instability, aneuploidy.
- Centrosome dysfunction promotes secretion of inflammatory factors → drivers of senescence and **tumor immune escape**.
