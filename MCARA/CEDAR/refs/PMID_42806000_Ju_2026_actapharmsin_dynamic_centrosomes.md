## Key content
1. Centrosome = evolutionarily conserved MTOC with **cell type-specific functions**; in somatic cells regulates chromosome segregation, **asymmetric cell division**, **ciliogenesis**.
2. Centrosomes can undergo **extensive remodeling or complete elimination** (oogenesis, spermatogenesis, neurodevelopment).
3. **Core mechanism (oogenesis):** CDK1-mediated phosphorylation of **SAS-6** disrupts its phase separation → **cartwheel disassembly** → centriole elimination.
4. Dysregulated centrosome dynamics → cancer, neurodevelopmental disorders, infertility. Centrosome regulators = therapeutic targets.

## Relevance to CEDAR / MCARA
- ⭐ **Regulated ratchet:** the cell has active "reset" mechanisms (SAS-6/CDK1, elimination) — CEDAR is a *regulated*, not passive, ratchet.
- ⭐ Supports the **acentriolar window** concept (ERC "Eliminate → Rebuild"): elimination of centrioles is a natural, reversible cell program.
