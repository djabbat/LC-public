## Key content
- Comprehensive review of **centriole elimination** — the active removal of centrioles in specialized cell types.
- Context: oogenesis (C. elegans), early embryos, and other systems.
- Discusses molecular drivers (including SAS-6/CDK1 axes) and physiological meaning.

## Relevance
- Direct support for the **acentriolar window** (ERC "Eliminate → Rebuild") and the "regulated ratchet" framing of CEDAR.
- Establishes elimination as a normal, programmed cell behavior (not only pathology).
