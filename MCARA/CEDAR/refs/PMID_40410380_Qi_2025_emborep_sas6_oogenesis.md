## Key content
- **Primary source** of the CDK1–SAS-6–cartwheel mechanism reviewed in Ju 2026 (PMID 42806000).
- Dynamic phosphorylation of SAS-6 controls both **centrosome duplication** and **elimination** during C. elegans oogenesis.
- Phosphorylation disrupts SAS-6 phase separation → cartwheel disassembly → centriole elimination.

## Relevance
- Molecular basis of the **acentriolar window** / centriole reset.
- Connects cell-cycle kinase (CDK1) control to centriole architecture → MCARA cross-counter coupling (#2 ↔ cell cycle).
