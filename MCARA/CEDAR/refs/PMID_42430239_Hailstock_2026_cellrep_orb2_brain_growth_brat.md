## Abstract (Europe PMC, сокращено)
> Neurodevelopment requires precise translational control, the disruption of which is implicated in neurological disorders, developmental delays, and microcephaly. We identify a role for the Drosophila CPEB-family protein Orb2, a translational regulator, in controlling brain size in a dose-dependent manner. Loss of orb2 results in larval brain hypotrophy, whereas orb2 overexpression causes brain overgrowth. … Structure-function analysis reveals that Orb2 RNA-binding activity promotes brain growth, while its poly-Q and ZZ domains restrain overgrowth. … orb2 functions upstream of the translational repressor Brain tumor (Brat), modulating Brat protein levels and, consequently, influencing brain size.
