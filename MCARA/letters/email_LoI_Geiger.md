I hope this finds you well. A quick update on the EIC Pathfinder proposal (deadline 28 October).

The consortium: GLA (PI), Pierre Gönczy (EPFL, C. elegans), Wolfgang Wagner (RWTH, epigenetic), Anu Suomalainen (Helsinki, mitochondria), Maria Magiera (Institut Curie, U-ExM). Six countries.
