Dear Prof. Rattan,

I am writing regarding my manuscript «The Multi-Counter Architecture of Organismal Aging: A Quantitative Framework for Integrating Mechanistic Theories» (ID: 7cc6de62), submitted to Biogerontology on 3 June 2026, which received a transfer recommendation on 14 July 2026.

I have reviewed the suggested transfer journals, but I respectfully request that the manuscript be reconsidered at Biogerontology. The journal is the right home for this work for three reasons:

1. **Scope fit.** MCARA proposes a quantitative framework integrating mechanistic theories of aging — this is exactly the interdisciplinary gerontology that Biogerontology publishes. The journal has published similar theoretical frameworks (e.g., Kowald & Kirkwood, 2016; Gladyshev, 2014).

2. **The manuscript was revised.** The original submission was rewritten as narrative-first, with a clear Figure 1, a 13-group experimental design table, and explicit falsifiable predictions — addressing the likely concerns of the initial review.

3. **Subscription model.** I am not requesting open access publication. Standard subscription-model publication incurs no article processing charge, which is necessary for our unfunded independent research group (Georgia Longevity Alliance, NGO).

If the editorial concern was about fit rather than quality, I would be grateful for specific guidance on what aspect of the manuscript falls outside the journal's scope, and I am prepared to revise accordingly.
