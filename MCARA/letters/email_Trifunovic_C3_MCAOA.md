Dear Professor Trifunovic,

I am putting together an EIC Pathfinder consortium (deadline 28 October 2026) and would like to ask whether the Mitochondrial Counter role fits your group's expertise.

The project — MCARA: Five independent counters of replicative aging, measured in parallel on the same cells. Human fibroblasts (BJ-hTERT, 2% O₂). With active telomerase and physiological hypoxia, telomeres are protected and ROS is minimal — yet the cells eventually stop dividing. The centriole is the leading candidate for the limiting factor, but all five counters must be tested.

C3 — Mitochondrial Counter: mtDNA damage (qPCR), ROS (MitoSOX), and Seahorse respirometry on the same cells at three passage points (P25, P35, P45) and at arrest. Your lab's deep expertise in mitochondrial function and aging — mtDNA maintenance, OXPHOS, mitochondrial stress responses — is a natural fit for this role.

Consortium so far: GLA (Georgia, PI), Pierre Gönczy (EPFL, C. elegans centriole), Wolfgang Wagner (RWTH, epigenetic clock), Maria Magiera (Institut Curie, U-ExM / tubulin), Hartmut Geiger (Ulm, HSC mouse model). Six countries.
